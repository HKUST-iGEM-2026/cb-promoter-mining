> **Standard URL:** `2026.igem.wiki/hkust/human-practices`
> **Medal relevance:** Silver (required — Human Practices)

> **Placeholder — to be completed by the team.** iGEM asks *how your work is responsible and good for the
> world*, grounded in engagement with people affected by or expert in your project. Below is a scaffold
> tailored to this project; replace each prompt with what the team actually did.

## Why this matters for our project

Our project lowers the barrier to **engineering *Clostridium butyricum*** — a probiotic and industrial
organism. That has clear upside (better therapeutics, greener chemical production) and real
responsibilities (engineering a gut-relevant, spore-forming anaerobe). Human Practices is where we show
we engaged with that seriously.

## Stakeholders & experts to engage

> **Prompt.** Who did you talk to, and how did it change your project? Candidates for this project:
> - Microbiologists / *Clostridium* researchers (is a computational parts catalogue actually useful to
>   them? what would make it usable?)
> - Synthetic biologists who work in non-model chassis (what parts do they most lack?)
> - Biosafety / biosecurity experts (engineering a gut anaerobe; spore-formers)
> - Bioinformaticians / ML-for-genomics researchers (is our evaluation honest and our method sound?)
> - Potential end users of the promoter catalogue (other iGEM teams, labs)

## Integrated Human Practices

> **Prompt.** Give concrete examples where stakeholder input **changed a design decision** — e.g. how
> feedback shaped which promoters we prioritized, how we framed the deliverable (candidate list vs
> "map"), our licensing choices, or our validation plan. Integration (not just consultation) is what
> earns credit.

## Responsibility & the honest-framing decision

One built-in responsibility choice worth documenting: we deliberately frame the output as an **enriched,
FDR-controlled candidate list requiring wet-lab validation, not a definitive promoter map** — because
over-claiming precision would mislead downstream users. Expand on the reasoning and any expert input
behind this.
