## Angles that fit this project

Our project is unusually well-suited to explaining a few ideas the public rarely sees:

- **"How do you read a genome with AI?"** — a plain-language explainer of promoters, why AT-rich genomes
  are hard, and how machine learning helps (the "AT-trap" is an intuitive hook).
- **Honest science / evaluating models** — why "good on a test" isn't "good in the real world," using our
  balanced-vs-genome-scale finding as a teaching example about not fooling yourself with metrics.
- **Open science** — we published our models and data; explain why reproducibility and reuse matter.
