## Dry-lab (computational) safety

Our core work is computational and involves **no biological materials, no pathogens, and no wet-lab
hazards**. Relevant considerations we can speak to:

- **Data & tools** are public and used under their licenses (see [Attributions](attributions.md)).
- **Responsible outputs.** We predict *promoters* (expression control elements), not toxins, virulence
  factors, or any hazard-enabling sequence. We frame outputs as candidates for validation, not
  definitive claims, to avoid misleading downstream use.
- **No dual-use sequence design.** The pipeline finds regulatory elements in an existing genome; it does
  not design pathogens or enhance any organism's harmful traits.

## Wet-lab (organism) safety

> **Prompt — wet-lab team + biosafety officer to complete.**

- **Chassis:** *Clostridium butyricum* (type strain DSM 10702). It is a spore-forming, strictly
  anaerobic bacterium; many strains are used as **probiotics**.
- **Important strain-provenance note to verify:** *C. butyricum* is generally low-risk, but **some
  environmental strains can carry the gene for botulinum neurotoxin type E.** Document that your working
  strain is a **non-toxigenic** industrial/type strain from a reputable culture collection, and confirm
  its risk classification with your institution and local regulations. **Do not treat this page's
  statement as a clearance — verify it.**
- **Containment & handling:** biosafety level, anaerobic culture practices, waste/decontamination, and
  spore handling to be documented.
- **Genetic modifications:** what constructs (promoter–reporter fusions) and vectors were used, and why
  they are safe and appropriately contained.
