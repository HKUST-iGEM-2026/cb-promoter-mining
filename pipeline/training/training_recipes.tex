\section{Shared training contract}
Training uses weighted cross-entropy:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=\frac{\sum_i w_i\,\mathrm{CE}(\hat y_i,y_i)}{\sum_i w_i}.
\end{equation}
Silver positives are putative promoter-positive upstream DNA labelled from predicted housekeeping-gene annotations and genomic position, not mapped TSS or experimentally measured promoters. In deployed T15\_xs, these housekeeping upstreams are training-only and receive sample-confidence weight 0.3 for training loss, not a biological probability; training negatives are rescaled to equal positive weight mass. The trainers select checkpoints on \emph{unweighted} validation average precision, not trapezoidal PR area. Early stopping retains the best checkpoint; it is not optimizer-state resumption or a demonstrated preemption-save trap. Seed 42 does not guarantee cross-runtime bit identity.

Warmup ramps the learning rate; linear schedules decay it after warmup. AdamW applies decoupled weight decay. Dropout is disabled during inference. These settings and mixed precision are recipe choices, not guarantees against overfitting.

\section{Standalone models}
\textbf{ProkBERT:} fine-tune the backbone and two-class head. Historical validation/test AP is 0.876/0.872.

\textbf{NT-v2 250M:} freeze the backbone, train query/value LoRA updates and the classifier. Rank 8, alpha 16 (scale 2), dropout 0.1; the run records 0.507\% trainable parameters and no gradient checkpointing. Historical validation/test AP is 0.853/0.841. This path needs its separate Transformers-4.x environment and is not deployed.

\textbf{One-hot CNN:} 81-by-4 input, convolution widths 64/128/256, kernels 7/5/3, BatchNorm and max pooling, then adaptive global max pooling, FC 128 and two logits. Gradient clipping is norm 1.0; cosine warm restarts use T0=5 and Tmult=2. Historical validation/test AP is 0.811/0.818. This is not fusion CNN-v2.

\section{Deployed variants}
Optuna subprocess sweeps maximize each trainer's saved validation AP; short trials require winner retraining. The deployed \texttt{T21\_xs} ProkBERT uses LR 4.675283456079137e-05, batch 16, 10 epochs, warmup 0.11090738634337446, weight decay 0.07894770663194649, dropout 0.1025172750748932 and attention pooling. Recovered original-run validation/test AP is 0.8080683612857302/0.8249372466065321, not a remeasurement. Earlier plain-split test AP 0.884 is separate.

Fusion CNN-v2 adds ENAC width 5 and di-/trinucleotide branches to one-hot features, with filters 64/128/256 and fusion dimension 256. Recorded LR is 0.0003748224111698842, batch 32, dropout 0.10382857322260322, weight decay 0.00024777455052193346, 30 epochs, patience 8 and seed 42. Historical validation/test AP is 0.738/0.726.

The third member is a logistic probe on frozen Evo2-7B embeddings, not NT or CNN-v1. Current extraction mean-pools \texttt{blocks.28.mlp.l3} in float32 to 4096 features; exact historical extraction revision/layer remains unresolved. StandardScaler and LogisticRegression(max\_iter=5000) select against RF on weighted validation AP, then refit 44,085 train plus 3,052 validation rows. Historical validation/test AP is 0.7007/0.7026.

\section{Provenance and use}
The deployed validation/test sets have 547 positives among 3,052/3,053 rows, about 18\%, not balanced. All members retain 888 training-only \emph{B. licheniformis} windows extracted from GCF\_000011645.1/\allowbreak{}NC\_006270.3 instead of source-compatible GCF\_000008425.1/\allowbreak{}NC\_006322.1.

Whole-cluster splits reduce one leakage route, not all sequence dependence. Benchmark strains supplied silver (annotation/position-derived putative positive) and putative-negative training windows while published TSS labels were withheld; neither label establishes measured promoter activity or absence. The deployed mine averages raw-score ranks on shared coordinates without calibration; a high mean is not unanimity or probability. Restricted benchmark proxies do not establish biological purity.

Current commands are in \texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}PIPELINE.md}, loading in \texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}MODELS.md}, and cards under \texttt{release/\allowbreak{}huggingface/}. DNA and motif labels require experimental testing.
