## Method

The step-1 full-genome scan emitted exact logit1−logit0 values, then applied a score+AT Platt map. Full/restricted tables reuse that scan; the model-independent restriction is 300 bp upstream/50 bp into annotated genes. This is not the deployed step-10 benchmark.

Same-strand 81-bp windows expand ±40 bp to cover TSS; unmatched starts merge at ≤81 bp. The precision proxy is covered TSS/(covered TSS+unmatched peaks), not matched-call precision. The historical region mask includes exact window-end/region-start touches. Full scope has 9,259,970 windows, 1,245 coverable starts and maximum recall 100%; restricted scope has 1,773,311 windows (19.2%), 1,234 coverable starts and maximum recall 99.12%. Genome lengths reported by the benchmark are 4,630,065/4,629,948 bp. TSS/window densities 0.0134/0.0702% are not biological priors.

**Silver positives** are putative promoter-positive upstream DNA labelled from predicted housekeeping-gene annotations and genomic position, not mapped TSS or experimentally measured promoters. Putative-negative windows are not experimentally established promoter-free DNA.

Calibration fits the 93:186 short-sequence mixture; prevalence/composition/context can shift. Exact emitted logits avoid the score-inversion saturation issue, but AT maps can reorder tracks. Strictly increasing score-only maps preserve ranks/ties only in exact arithmetic. Both benchmark strains supplied silver positives and putative-negative training windows, and truth is incomplete. FP means unmatched, not proven false promoter.
