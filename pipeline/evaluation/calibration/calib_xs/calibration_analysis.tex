\section{Calibration: calib\_xs}\label{calibration-calib_xs}

Historical four-model calibration, not the deployed ProkBERT/CNN-v2/Evo2 ensemble. The 279-row mixture has 93 T9 positives and 186 putative negatives (130 convergent/56 CDS). Original prediction CSVs/maps are absent; the T9 positives are 49--52 bp and the extractor defaults to 52 bp. Padding does not supply 81-bp biological context.

\subsection{Method}\label{method}

StandardScaler scales the selected features. LogisticRegression compares score-only and score+AT maps, each unregularized or L2, with 5-times-5 stratified OOF log-loss selection (tol 0.005, L2 C=1). This round chose unregularized score+AT. Lower ECE, Brier and log-loss is better; higher AUROC and AP is better. Plus/minus values are repeated-CV standard deviations, not confidence intervals.

\subsection{Interpretation}\label{interpretation}

The AT ablation lowers recorded OOF log-loss on this mixture. Aggregate improvements coexist with more rows worsening for some members. Intercept, score coefficient and AT coefficient change jointly; row errors do not isolate a causal AT effect. err is \textbar score\ensuremath{-}label\textbar; \ensuremath{\Delta} is calibrated error minus raw error, and a flip crosses the wrong side of 0.5.

The sampled prevalence and short context limit transfer. AT-dependent maps can change rank ensembles; the deployed mine uses raw ranks, not these maps. Reused OOF cohorts are sensitivity evidence, not independent final tests. \href{../CALIBRATION_NOTES.md}{Calibration notes} identify scripts and output prefixes.
