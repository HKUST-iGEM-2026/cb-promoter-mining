\section{Target FDR screening}\label{target-fdr-screening}

Historical T27 restricted target run, separate from the ordinary 929/180/45 catalogue. Candidate/null counts are 175,911/1,726. Mean member percentiles are computed over each combined candidate+null pool, not ordinary target ranks. The intergenic null is putative; exchangeability, exact composition matching and promoter-free status are not established.

\subsection{Screening rule}\label{screening-rule}

Window p = (count(null score \ensuremath{\geq} candidate score) + 1)/(Nnull + 1). Select p\ensuremath{\leq}0.05, merge same-strand windows, assign each peak its minimum selected p, then apply BH over selected peaks. Selection/minimum p-values do not establish calibrated peak-level or biological FDR\ensuremath{\leq}5\%. join\_fdr\_qvalues.py attaches the lowest overlapping same-strand peak q; it does not retest ordinary candidates.

\subsection{Recorded result}\label{recorded-result}

Null median/p95/p99/max is 0.6135/0.8911/0.9526/0.9944, minimum attainable p 5.79e-04. There are 3,358 screening loci at q\ensuremath{\leq}0.05 and the same count at q\ensuremath{\leq}0.10. Reported top 80 scores span 0.9964--0.9994, q 1.01e-02--1.01e-02; no summit sequence was truncated.

\subsection{Files and coordinates}\label{files-and-coordinates}

peaks\_fdr.csv stores all peaks; top80\_\allowbreak{}promoters.csv stores the reported sequence subset. The optional motif CSV is not committed here. summit is the 0-based forward-genome start of an 81-bp window, including on the reverse strand; sequence is in reading orientation, not a mapped TSS.

Reproduction needs aligned candidate \textbf{and} null raw member tracks on the same grid/settings. Stored peak tables alone cannot reconstruct pooled ranks/window p-values; a mean of raw null scores is not the pooled-rank null. Motifs and downstream gene associations need biological testing. \href{../../../../../docs/RESULTS.md\#interpreting-candidates-and-downstream-units}{Interpretation} defines the separate claims.
