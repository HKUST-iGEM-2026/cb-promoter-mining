Consolidated from \texttt{/project/igem2026/cb-promoter-mining/evaluation-and-calibration/T26/calib\_xs} --- 4 model(s): cnn, cnn\_v2, nt\_lora, prokbert. \textbf{Eval set:} 279 sequences --- 93 positives, 186 negatives (130 convergent-intergenic + 56 CDS-interior). Window 81 bp.

\subsection*{3. Does the AT covariate earn its place? (score-only vs score+AT)}

Recomputed from each CSV with a repeated-stratified-KFold logistic. \texttt{+AT} is worth keeping only if it lowers OOF log-loss; a \textbf{positive AT coef} means high-AT sequences are pushed *up* (AT-rich promoters rescued), negative means pushed *down*.

\begin{center}
\resizebox{\ifdim\width>\linewidth\linewidth\else\width\fi}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{lccccc}
\hline
Model & OOF log-loss score-only & OOF log-loss +AT & AT helps? & AT coef & mean P(positives) score-only $\to$ +AT \\
\hline
cnn & 0.5351 & 0.4689 & \textbf{yes} & +0.962 & 0.461 $\to$ 0.547 \\
cnn\_v2 & 0.5273 & 0.4684 & \textbf{yes} & +0.950 & 0.477 $\to$ 0.548 \\
nt\_lora & 0.6401 & 0.5791 & \textbf{yes} & +0.869 & 0.332 $\to$ 0.419 \\
prokbert & 0.5198 & 0.4552 & \textbf{yes} & +1.028 & 0.487 $\to$ 0.563 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}%
}
\end{center}

*Decision rule for the calibrator: keep +AT only where it lowers OOF log-loss; otherwise score-only (simpler) is the honest choice.*

\subsection*{5. Takeaways}

\begin{itemize}
\item AT \textbf{helps} (lower OOF log-loss, positive coef = rescues AT-rich positives) for: cnn, cnn\_v2, nt\_lora, prokbert.
\item The calibrator made the majority of rows worse than raw for: cnn\_v2, nt\_lora, prokbert --- that is the base-rate/intercept level-shift, not necessarily the AT term (see \S{}3).
\item Ensemble aggregation (T29) is rank-percentile and calibration-invariant, so these distortions matter only for per-model probabilities / fixed thresholds, not the ensemble ranking.
\end{itemize}

{\footnotesize \texttt{err = |probability - label|}; \texttt{$\Delta$ vs raw = calib\_err - raw\_err} (positive => calibration made the row worse). \S{}3 ablation is recomputed from raw\_logit + at\_fraction + label with a stdlib logistic (5x5 stratified OOF).}
